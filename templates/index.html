How to Play

You will memorize sets of words across 4 progressive difficulty levels. After memorizing each set, you'll see a series of probe words and decide how confident you are that each word was in your study list!

  • Confidence Rating Scale: Rate 1 (Definitely New) to 6 (Definitely Old).
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Hit number keys 1 to 6 on your keyboard for lightning fast responses!
